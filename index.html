PRIZEFIGHTER TERMS
Terms of Service

Prizefighter is a profile, search, social, advertising, plugin and merchandise platform for Gloved Boxing and Bare Knuckle Boxing / BKB only.

Users may register as fighters or fans. Public visitors may search and read public profile information, but posting, commenting, liking, messaging and other interactive features require a registered and verified account.

Fighter information, BoxRec links, Facebook profile links, sponsor details and uploaded media may be reviewed by Prizefighter admin before approval. False, misleading, abusive, illegal or non-boxing/BKB content may be rejected or removed.

Paid plugins, sponsorship placements and advertisements are controlled by contract/subscription status. Access may expire or be removed when payment fails, a contract ends, or admin rejects the submission.

This Phase 11 demo text is a launch-ready placeholder. Before public launch, replace it with final legal wording reviewed for the countries where Prizefighter operates.

PRIZEFIGHTER PRIVACY
Privacy Policy

Prizefighter collects account and profile information needed to create fighter and fan profiles, run search, verify users, manage plugins, process reports, handle advertising enquiries and support merchandise listings.

Public profile data may include name, ring name, city, country, sport, weight division, verification status, bio, sponsor display, posts and approved profile links. Private data should be stored securely in the production backend and only used for platform operation and admin review.

Payment processing should be handled through PayPal or another approved payment provider. Prizefighter should not store full card details in the app.

Users should be able to request account review, correction, deletion or removal of public profile information through the contact/admin process.

This Phase 11 demo text is a launch-ready placeholder. Final privacy wording must be reviewed before live public launch.

COMMUNITY RULES
Prizefighter Community Rules

Allowed sports: Gloved Boxing and Bare Knuckle Boxing / BKB only.

No fake profiles: fighter and fan accounts must use truthful information and may require admin verification.

No abuse: harassment, scams, spam, fake sponsors, fake records, impersonation and illegal content can be reported and removed.

BoxRec rule: BoxRec links must point to a real BoxRec fighter account only.

Facebook rule: fighter Facebook links must point to a personal Facebook profile, not a page or group.
